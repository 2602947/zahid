01 — OUR UNIVERSITY

More than a place to study.

Air University is a federally chartered public sector university established in 2002. Its main campus is situated in Sector E-9, Islamabad, near the Margalla foothills.

The university focuses on teaching, research, innovation and developing graduates with professional knowledge and a sense of responsibility.
